FAQ

Finance is a trust game. People follow you for the edge, but they only pay you when they believe you. So the system is built around one thing: content that shows how you think and turns that into conversations with buyers.

If you already have a solid offer and real expertise (trading education, investing, wealth coaching, financial planning), this will work for you too.

You stay the expert. We become your content team.

Your responsibilities:

  • A few hours, 1–2 days a month, to film
  • A monthly strategy and review call
  • Quick approvals so everything stays in your voice

Our responsibilities:

  • Research: we study the best performing finance content on Instagram.
  • Scripting: we script a full month of short form content, plus direction on what to film and how to batch it.
  • Editing & uploading: we edit, post, and send you basic reporting.

You’re not dumb. Social media is just a different beast. Most finance creators have posted consistently, seen a few posts hit and most flop, and have no idea why.

What we do:

  1. Research winning formats in your space and see what already works on your account.
  2. Post to find outliers (anything that does 5x your normal reach).
  3. Double down on the winners on purpose: same angles, same topics, new variations.

Clients. Unless it turns into revenue, it doesn’t matter. So we:

  • Create content that attracts buyers, not random views.
  • Use calls to action that send people to your DMs, your free resources, and your calendar.
  • Track milestones around leads and calls, not vanity metrics.

Building an audience that buys takes time to test, find your outliers, and scale what works. That’s why we work in months, not weeks.
